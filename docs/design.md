# Design Team 🎨 

We have a team of designers that do the “heavy lifting” of visuals for the conference, but this team handles everything else, from stickers and t-shirt designs to banners, badges, and lanyards.

## Some of the responsibilities include:
- Print (and where suitable also design) social stickers for the attendees, i.e. pronouns, hug/fist bump/stay away, etc. (We do have the templates from previous years available.)
- Design the t-shirts.
- Design banners, booth covers, and other materials with sponsors’ logos and the conference logo.


### You will fit well here if:
- [x] You have experience with design or would like to learn more. 
- [x] You like creating content for social or printed media.
