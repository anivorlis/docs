# Financial Aid Team (a.k.a. FinAid) 💌

The EuroPython Financial Aid Programme provides financial support to help people attend the conference. This team processes the requests for grants, which are open to anyone who needs help. Last year, the main criteria for judging grant applications were: participation in the community, socio-economic factors, and diversity.

## Some of the responsibilities include:
- Setting up the programme policies, guidelines, and timelines.
- Coordinating with other teams, such as programme and comms when needed.
- Coordinating the application review process, assuring that the team collectively performs enough reviews.
- Coordinating the processing of applications and reimbursements.
- Helping with answering questions in the Financial Aid help desk queue.
- Participating in the review and application decision process.
- Facilitating discussions to discuss policy, special cases, and other topics that come up.
