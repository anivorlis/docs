# Communications Team 🎙️

Handles all conference communications, which include social media, blog, newsletter posts, mailings, and mailing lists.

### Team responsibilities include:
- Write content about the EuroPython conference, including social media posts and blog articles.
- Review, edit and polish text.
- Responsible for the EuroPython brand strategy, and initiating and coordinating our marketing efforts.
- Keep the website content clear, up-to-date, and aligned with our brand strategy. 
- Manage media partners.
